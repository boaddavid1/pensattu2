Before you visit

Common questions.

What should I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable in. You'll see everything from suits to jeans — there's no dress code here.

Is there something for my kids?

Yes — our Kids Ministry runs age-appropriate programming during both Sunday services, with trained volunteers and a secure check-in system.

Where do I park?

Free on-site parking is available, with overflow parking and directions posted on Sunday mornings.

Can I watch online if I can't make it in person?

Absolutely — both services stream live, and every message is archived on our Sermons page afterward.

How do I join a community group?

Fill out the form above and select "Joining a community group" — our Community Groups Lead will reach out to match you with one nearby.
